About us

We are a team of passionate individuals committed to addressing the issue of food waste in India. Our goal is to create a system that connects food donors with charities and NGOs, while also reducing the environmental impact of food waste.


We're passionate about reducing food waste and making a positive impact on our planet. Our Waste Food Management System is designed to streamline food waste reduction efforts, minimize environmental impact, and maximize resource efficiency.


Our mission is to tackle the global issue of food waste through innovative technology solutions. Our Waste Food Management System empowers businesses and individuals to track, reduce, and repurpose food waste. By optimizing food supply chains and promoting sustainable practices, we aim to create a more sustainable future.


Join us in our mission to reduce food waste and build a more sustainable future. Let's make a difference together.
