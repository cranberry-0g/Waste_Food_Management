# Food Waste Management 🍽️

**Food Donate** is a simple and meaningful web-based platform designed to reduce food waste by connecting food donors like hotels, restaurants, and event organizers with people in need. 
This project promotes community participation in donating surplus food to prevent waste and fight hunger.

---

## Features

- 🏠 Clean and responsive homepage
- 📝 Food donation form for quick and easy contributions
- 🖼️ Image gallery showcasing our social impact
- 🚚 Door pickup service for convenience
- 📄 About and Contact pages
- 🔗 Social media integration
